# BRIEF.md — allamc (Stage 0 · approved by the owner 2026-09-23, D-88)

**One line:** Independent service businesses in India can sell AMCs, deliver every service visit from the
technician's phone, and bill with GST in one simple app — so no visit, report or renewal is missed.

**Paying customer:** owner of an independent multi-brand service business.
**Daily users:** office staff/dispatcher, technicians (budget Android, often Hindi-first), the owner.
**First segment (proposed, VALIDATE — D-81):** AC / RO / appliance service businesses with 2–30
technicians in one city. The product stays category-configurable (D-02); only the *first customers* are
narrowed.

**Core loop (the one job that must work end to end):**
sell an AMC contract → the visit is scheduled or logged → the technician completes it on the phone →
the customer receives the service report → the business invoices with GST.
*(Renewal reminders close the loop and come right after.)*

**What they use today instead:** WhatsApp groups, phone calls, paper job cards, Excel or a notebook for
contracts, and Vyapar / Tally / handwritten bills for invoicing.

**Why now:** cheap Android phones and UPI are everywhere, and customers expect digital service reports;
most AMC businesses still run on memory and WhatsApp, which is how PM visits and renewals get missed.

**Success in 6 months (from Stage 3 start):** 5 paying businesses; ≥ 80% of their visits closed in the app
with proof; ≥ 1 renewal per business that they say they would have missed.

## Riskiest assumptions (ranked; each is a VALIDATE decision)
1. **Adoption & payment (D-60, D-61):** small owners will move off WhatsApp/Excel and pay per technician
   per month.
2. **Technicians will really use it (D-33, D-35, D-55):** checklist + photos + customer proof on a budget
   phone, in Hindi, without slowing them down.
3. **The pain that sells is missed visits and renewals (D-56):** strong enough to switch for.
4. **allamc can be their only billing system (D-40):** owners trust it for GST invoices instead of
   Vyapar/Tally; a CA confirms formats.
5. **Starter templates are enough at first (D-10, D-11):** owners won't need the no-code builder to start.
6. **Offline is needed often (D-34):** frequency of no-signal sites among target users.

## Out of scope until Stage 3 is passed
OEM service-centre features · marketplace · consumer app · WhatsApp Cloud API (one-tap links only) ·
e-mandate auto-renewal · contract amendments · full inventory and purchasing · custom roles ·
e-invoicing (IRN) · multi-branch · the no-code builder (starter templates only) · Excel import (assisted
by us instead).
