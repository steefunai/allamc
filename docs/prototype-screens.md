# Stage 1 prototype — core-loop screens (throwaway; English + Hindi)

The clickable prototype covers only the core loop. Each screen lists the task a test user will attempt.

| # | Screen | Who | Test task |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Sign-up + 3-step setup (business, GST, first technician) | Owner | "Set up your business" |
| P2 | Owner home: today's jobs, contracts expiring in 30/60/90 days | Owner | "Which renewals are due this month?" |
| P3 | Customers: one search box (phone/name) + add customer, site, AC unit from a template | Office | "Add Mr Sharma's two ACs" |
| P4 | Sell AMC: pick units → template (Comprehensive / Non-comprehensive) → price → dates → review | Office | "Sell Mr Sharma a 1-year AMC" |
| P5 | New job in ≤ 3 taps (phone lookup → unit → issue) + assign with suggestions | Office | "Log a breakdown call and assign it" |
| P6 | Technician "My day" (ordered jobs, call, navigate) | Technician | "Start your next job" |
| P7 | Job execution: check-in, one checklist step per screen, photo, voice note | Technician | "Do the PM service" |
| P8 | Close job: summary, customer OTP or signature | Technician | "Finish the job with the customer" |
| P9 | Service report as the customer sees it (WhatsApp link) | Customer | "What was done on your AC?" |
| P10 | GST invoice: preview, share link, mark paid (UPI/cash) | Office/Owner | "Bill the AMC and record payment" |

Test with at least 2 owners/office staff and 3 technicians. For technicians, test on a budget Android
phone in Hindi. Record sessions with `docs/feedback/_TEMPLATE.md`.
